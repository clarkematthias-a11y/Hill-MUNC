Welcome

The inaugural HILLMUNC

HILLMUNC is designed to give high school students a challenging, engaging, and welcoming Model United Nations experience—with a mix of General Assembly, specialized, and crisis-style committees.

DELEGATES

Build your voice

Practice diplomacy, public speaking, negotiation, research, and resolution writing in a student-focused environment.

COMMITTEES

Find your challenge

Choose from conventional committees and faster-paced crisis experiences so delegates can find a format that fits their interests.

COMMUNITY

Meet other students

HILLMUNC is built around collaboration, friendly competition, and connections with students from across the region.
